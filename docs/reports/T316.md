## Why 12 present-but-unverified tasks were left open

T060, T072–T075, T117, T118, T120, T193, T201, T211, T212, T215. Their named files exist. That is the
only thing a file-existence check proves, and T316's criterion is "present **and correct**".

Two of them name a file to **modify** rather than a deliverable to create — T211
`AuthorizationService`, T212 `TransactionService` — so for those, presence is not evidence of anything
at all. I did spot-check two: T211's `AuthorizationService` does handle `WorkflowStage::Correction`
(line 25), and T212's `updateTransaction()` does read `$transaction->revision()` for numbering and
supersession. Neither was enough to certify "correct" without comparing against the behaviour the task
complains about, and stopping at "looks plausible" is the failure mode of an audit.

Each needs one behavioural check — read the method, or run the named test. That is a task-sized unit of
work, not a checkbox, and the alternative was to mark twelve boxes on a guess.

## A methodology error, caught and corrected

The first pass reported **71** absent deliverables. It was wrong about 15 of them: it compared
backticked paths literally, so a task naming `plan.md` or `DecisionForm.vue` — a bare filename that
exists under a directory — was reported missing. Re-running with basenames resolved against
`git ls-files` brought it to 56. Every number in the table above is from the second pass.

This is the T254 defect reproduced in a new place: an assertion that cannot fail, in the opposite
direction. A reconciliation that over-reports absence would have had 15 tasks investigated for nothing.

## Known limitations

- **The 12 unverified tasks are unverified, not verified-and-passed.** They are the largest remaining
  pool of cheap wins, and the next agent should finish them with one behavioural check each rather than
  re-deriving this table.
- **The 44 no-path lines were grouped, not individually adjudicated.** They are umbrella tasks
  (T150–T158 implement whole phases; T205–T209 are "implement Phase N per tasks.md"; T312–T315 are this
  session's own convergence output). T205–T209 are structurally duplicated by the US-level tasks they
  delegate to, which is a structural observation rather than a verdict on their content.
- **Path existence is not behaviour.** The whole basis of the 56 is "no file of that name exists", which
  for a task like T093 is close to conclusive, and for one like T124 is weaker than it looks — a service
  could satisfy the requirement under a different name.
- T109 remains blocked on the font asset; T275 and T304 on maintainer decisions.
