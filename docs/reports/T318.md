# T318 — Reconcile the DELETE replay contradiction

**Status:** complete

## Maintainer decision

> DELETE operations are repeatable commands and are exempt from `X-PettyCash-Command-ID`.

Applied as:

- remove the `CommandId` requirement from `DELETE /transactions/{transactionId}`;
- remove the `CommandId` requirement from `DELETE /evidence/{evidenceId}`;
- do not wrap either DELETE in `IdempotencyService`;
- preserve the non-repeatable-command rule in `contracts/workflow.md`;
- make evidence deactivation effect-idempotent — a replay must not rewrite `deactivatedAt`.

T317 and T258 were not reopened or widened. FR-042 is unchanged: it lists "recording a decision,
changing workflow state, creating a revision, and recording final financial consumption", and a
pre-submission draft delete is none of them, so it never reached this path.

## Verification detail

Two things were settled by measurement rather than assumption:

- **The pre-existing failure.** `testGetEvidenceContentReturns200WithStream` fails on the untouched
  baseline. Had it not been checked, it would have been misattributed to this change.
- **The exemption is enforced on the artifact, not just the code.** `DeleteCommandIdExemptionTest`
  reads `ocs-api.yaml` as text, because the container PHP has no `yaml` extension
  (`yaml_parse_file()` returns `false`) and no YAML library is vendored. Each operation's block is
  bounded at the next verb key — the unbounded search T318 recorded produced two false positives whose
  `CommandId` belonged to a sibling `POST`/`PATCH` on the same path, and an unbounded `strpos` would
  reproduce them.

One authored test was wrong on its first run and was corrected rather than adjusted to pass: the
first-delete assertion compared a `$seeded` fixture against a value the service had just mutated
through the in-memory repository's shared instance. It now asserts the pre-state first, which makes
the post-state assertion mean something.

## Known limitations

- `DELETE /transactions/{id}` replay still answers 422 rather than a "already deleted" success. Out of
  scope: the decision was about the header, and any alternative answer is a new contract decision.
- `testGetEvidenceContentReturns200WithStream` remains red. Pre-existing, unrelated, not fixed here.
- The two DELETEs are now asymmetric under replay — the hard delete 404s on a re-read while the soft
  delete returns 200. That asymmetry follows from the two operations' different physical effects, not
  from an inconsistency in the exemption.
